[GOVERN 1.1] AI Governance Policies and Risk Tolerance
[SAMPLE – NOT OFFICIAL TEXT] Legal and regulatory requirements involving AI are understood, managed, and integrated into organizational policies. Risk management processes are aligned with organizational risk appetite and enterprise risk strategies.

[MAP 1.2] Contextual Scope and Impact Assessment
[SAMPLE – NOT OFFICIAL TEXT] The AI system's target users, societal context, deployment environment, and potential for direct and indirect negative impacts on individuals and communities are mapped prior to deployment.

[MEASURE 2.1] Test, Evaluation, Verification, and Validation (TEVV)
[SAMPLE – NOT OFFICIAL TEXT] AI systems are formally evaluated for accuracy, robustness, reliability, security, bias, and privacy protection using measurable metrics across representative test sets.

[MANAGE 3.1] Risk Treatment and Continuous Monitoring
[SAMPLE – NOT OFFICIAL TEXT] Risks identified during mapping and measurement are mitigated, transferred, or avoided through continuous real-time monitoring, incident response protocols, and automated circuit breakers.
