- **Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689)** (Official Journal of the European Union, July 2024)
- **NIST Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)** (National Institute of Standards and Technology, US Department of Commerce)

> **Official Text Notice**: The sample summaries shipped within `/corpus/` are non-official, educational, and research representations labelled `SAMPLE – NOT OFFICIAL TEXT`. They are intended exclusively for offline demonstration and testing. Production deployments must ingest authentic statutory texts obtained directly from official government Gazette notifications.
